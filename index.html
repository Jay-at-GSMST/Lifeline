About LIFELINE

LIFELINE is designed to make it easier for people to find support with everyday needs and difficult situations.

The website organizes resources into categories such as food, housing, education, jobs, transportation, technology, legal help, and health.

Resource information should be checked before relying on it. Services, hours, eligibility rules, and contact information can change.
